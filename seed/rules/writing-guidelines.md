# Resume Writing Guidelines

Use these rules when drafting resumes, cover letters, and self-introduction
documents for this project.

This file is the **universal principles layer**. The owner's personal style
ledger `feedback-rules.md` sits on top of it: on conflict, the ledger's
active rules win (the no-fabrication rule always stays supreme).

## Core Principles

- Start from the target role and job posting, then select only relevant
  evidence.
- Prefer specific actions and outcomes over personality claims.
- Use the candidate's verified facts from `profile.md` and
  `experience-bank.md`.
- Do not fabricate dates, metrics, company names, tools, responsibilities, or
  awards.
- If evidence is missing, write a placeholder such as `[성과 지표 확인 필요]`.

## Korean Writing Style

Use this section for Korean output. If the target document must be written in
English, preserve the same evidence-first structure but use direct professional
English and avoid literal translation from Korean.

- Use concise, professional Korean.
- Keep paragraphs focused on one claim and one evidence thread.
- Avoid inflated expressions such as "최고의", "완벽한", "무조건", unless the source
  evidence supports them.
- Prefer active, concrete verbs: 개선했다, 구현했다, 자동화했다, 분석했다,
  조율했다, 검증했다.
- Keep honorifics and endings consistent across the document.

## Resume Bullets

Strong bullets should follow this shape:

```text
[문제/목표]를 위해 [행동]을 수행했고, [결과/수치/영향]을 만들었다.
```

Examples of useful evidence:

- Performance improvement
- Cost or time reduction
- Reliability or quality improvement
- Automation impact
- User, business, or operational outcome
- Cross-functional coordination

## Cover Letter / Self-Introduction

Use this structure unless the application form dictates otherwise:

1. Why this company / role
2. Relevant experience and proof
3. How the candidate will contribute
4. Closing fit statement

Avoid generic company praise. Tie company research to the candidate's actual
experience.

## Output Format

- For web forms, prioritize the form's character limits and field prompts over
  A4 layout guidance.
- For plain text, keep paragraphs short and preserve source evidence.
- For HTML, PDF, or print-ready artifacts, follow `../../DESIGN.md`.

## Editing Checklist

- Every major claim has source evidence.
- The document is tailored to the target role.
- Important keywords from the job posting appear naturally.
- No unsupported metrics or exaggerated claims.
- Korean line breaks and paragraph lengths remain readable for the target
  medium.
- Visual styling follows `../../DESIGN.md` when creating visual or print-ready
  output.
